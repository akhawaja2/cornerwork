## First conversation

Ask for 15 minutes with the person who manages adult coaching and member follow-up. Show the four-step flow, then ask:

1. Tell me about the last member question that got lost after class. Where did it arrive, who owned it, and what happened?
2. What do coaches already do in Gymdesk, texts or WhatsApp? Can you show the actual workflow using non-sensitive examples?
3. Who would clear this queue, and when? Would that time be paid?
4. Would another coach read a brief before class? What would make them ignore it?
5. If this worked, would the gym pay $50–100/month? What would have to be true? Ask separately whether they would sell a $10 add-on to members.

Record current behavior before showing features. A polite positive reaction is weak evidence; a coach using it without reminders and an owner agreeing to a paid pilot are stronger signals. No offer, price, reply SLA or launch date has been committed by this research.
