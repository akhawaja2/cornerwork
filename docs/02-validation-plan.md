# 02 — Validation plan (90 days)

## Gates
| Stage | When | Do | Pass | Kill / rethink |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Wk 0 | Capital One policy check · trademark search · 10DLC registration · export attendance baselines from any pilot gym | — | Policy blocks it → stop |
| 1 | Wks 1–3 | Facebook post + 15 owner/coach interviews | ≥5 describe the pain unprompted | "Cool idea," no stories |
| 2 | Wks 2–6 | Concierge pilot with boxing coach (5–8 athletes, second iPhone line, LLM run by hand) | ≥50% still logging wk 4; coach replies ≥80% within 48h | Coach late by wk 2; <30% logging |
| 3 | Wks 4–8 | Pitch 5 gyms outside MK; pre-sell the add-on | 3 founding gyms; ≥5% of members offered buy it (target 10%) | 0 paid; owners want it free forever |
| 4 | Wks 6–12 | Waitlist attendance test pooled across founding gyms | +15% check-ins vs waitlist | No difference |
| 5 | Day 90 | Go / no-go | 3 gyms paying, 10+ members paying, coaches replying at 50/50 | Otherwise stop or pivot |

## Metrics (all from the `events` table)
- % opted-in athletes logging ≥1×/week
- Coach reply rate and median reply time (target ≤48h)
- Coach minutes/week per 20 athletes (target ≤120)
- Add-on opt-in % of members offered
- Check-ins/month vs each athlete's own 8–12 week baseline; treatment vs waitlist (difference-in-differences)
- 90-day retention, coached vs waitlist (needs ~140/group → multi-gym, later)
- Sean Ellis: "How disappointed if it went away?" ≥40% "very"

Power math: attendance +2 check-ins/mo (SD ~4) needs ~60/group; retention +10pp needs ~140/group.

## Where to find coaches/owners
- Facebook: Martial Arts Gym Owners United; Martial Arts Gym Owners
- USA Muaythai memberships; WTBA affiliate schools list
- Amateur shows / tournaments (USA Boxing Metro, IKF, WKA, local Muay Thai cards) — corner coaches, in person
- Own circle outside MK: boxing coach, wrestling club coaches (interviews, not sales)
- Avoid: Renzo Gracie affiliates (word travels to MK); gym-software companies' communities (copy risk)

## Facebook post (research, not selling)
> **Coaches & gym owners: what's the most annoying part of running your gym that nobody's fixed yet?**
> I'm a Muay Thai fighter (9-1-1) and a software engineer researching the problems coaches deal with outside of teaching. Not selling anything.
> 1. Between classes — how do you keep track of what each person is working on, or whether they're carrying an injury?
> 2. Communication — do members message you after class? Helpful or overwhelming?
> 3. Admin — what eats your time that isn't coaching?
> 4. Tools — what software do you pay for now, and what have you cancelled and why?
> Or tell me what frustrates you most that I didn't list. Comment or DM — I'd appreciate 10 minutes on a call.

Short version: *Muay Thai fighter + software engineer, researching not selling. Coaches/owners: what's the most frustrating part of running your gym outside of coaching? Retention, tracking progress, injuries, admin, software? 10 min call?*

## Interview script (Mom Test — past behavior, not opinions)
1. Last time a member quit, did you see it coming? What happened?
2. How do you track what each athlete is working on between classes? Show me.
3. Do members text you after class? What do you do with it?
4. Last injury a member had — how did you find out, and what changed in class?
5. What software do you pay for? What did you last cancel, and why?
6. Have you ever charged extra for more personal coaching? How did it go?
7. If a coach spent an hour a week replying to members for $150/mo, would your coaches do it? Which ones?
Never pitch in the interview. Show the design only 1:1 to someone who could be a customer.

## Outreach lines
- Gym owner: *"I'm a nationally ranked Muay Thai fighter and a software engineer. I'm building a tool that gives your members coaching between classes — which you charge for. Looking for 3 founding gyms; our share is waived for 3 months."*
